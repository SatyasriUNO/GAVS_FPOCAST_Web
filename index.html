Tech4Bharat · Problem Statement 6.4

Know your harvest before you sign the contract.

FPOCAST 2.0 uses drone surveys and AI to forecast how much an FPO will harvest, what grade it will be and when — with an honest confidence range, so buyers and FPOs can plan supply they can trust.

The problem

Processors can’t plan around supply that FPOs can’t predict

Farmer Producer Organisations make supply commitments from rough estimates. Buyers find out the real quantity and grade only when produce reaches the collection point.

HOW MUCH?

Volume is guessed

Commitments rest on estimates made at sowing, from acreage and last season.

WHAT GRADE?

Quality shows up late

Grade becomes clear only at the collection point — after the contract is signed.

WHEN?

Harvest timing shifts

Weather and irrigation move the harvest window, and logistics with it.

What it costs: broken contracts, penalties, distress sales of surplus, and buyers who quietly go elsewhere.

Evidence

Five real cases, one pattern

Different crops and states, same root cause: supply was committed without credible information on quantity, grade and timing.

How it works

From a drone flight to a contract decision

One drone platform serves every crop. The operator picks the crop model, the drone surveys the fields, and the server turns images into a supply forecast.

1

Pick the crop model

In the Model Selector. The server sends it to the drone’s Raspberry Pi 5, which loads and tests it.

2

Survey the fields

The drone flies a lawnmower path, capturing GPS-tagged images. Crop vision runs on board.

3

Forecast on the server

Yield, quality grade, harvest time and uncertainty — per field, then added up for the FPO.

4

Check the contract

Compare forecast supply with a buyer’s quantity, grade and date. The FPO board decides.

Key innovation · uncertainty-aware forecasting

Not “100 tonnes.”
100 tonnes, 85–115, with the risk stated.

Every forecast comes with a calibrated 90% range and the probability of falling short of what the buyer needs — and accounts for shared weather risk across nearby farms.

Architecture

Six layers, built on our patent-filed drone platform

FPOCAST reuses the drone, server and app structure of SAADAXA, our patent-filed Drone–Server–App system. Disaster-specific parts are replaced with agricultural intelligence.

1 · Data acquisitionDrone RGB + GPS images · farmer records · weather · historical yield and rejections
2 · ProcessingImage preprocessing · GPS field association · cleaning · feature extraction
3 · AI intelligence engineCrop vision · yield · quality grade · harvest time · uncertainty and risk
4 · Supply intelligenceFPO aggregation · grade-wise supply · buyer matching · contract feasibility
5 · BackendFastAPI · PostgreSQL + PostGIS · image storage · MLflow model tracking
6 · Decision supportField, FPO and buyer reports for FPO management
